The uncomfortable part

You have the skill.
Why does selling it feel so uncomfortable?

These are the sales moments this playbook helps you prepare for and handle more deliberately.

You hesitate to post your offer.

You don't want people to think you're disturbing them or trying too hard.

You feel nervous saying your price.

You start explaining, apologising or reducing your fee before the client even objects.

You don't know what to say after “How much?”

You send the price, the conversation goes quiet and you don't know how to continue.

You avoid following up.

You don't want to sound desperate, so interested prospects slip away without a clear next step.

Your talent is not the thing to apologise for. You deserve to confidently explain what you do, what it solves and what it costs.

A different way to see selling

Selling is how you make the value of your skill clear.

You identify a real need, explain how your service addresses it, agree on the work and price, then make the next step clear. No pressure tactics. No apologising for having a price.

By applying the playbook, you can work toward a clear sales sequence: identify the right prospect, explain the value, present the offer and price, respond to concerns, follow up and ask for the decision. That is how you move from simply having a skill to selling it deliberately.

Inside the playbook

Build a sales process you can actually use with your next prospect.

Use the lessons to make your offer clearer, your conversations more purposeful and your follow-up more organised.

01 / POSITIONING

Find the right people

Identify the kind of client you want to serve, the problem they need solved and why your service is relevant to them.

02 / VALUE

Explain why your work matters

Explain the practical value of your work so clients hear more than a list of skills or deliverables.

03 / OFFER

Make your offer clear

Put your scope, deliverables, price, process and terms into an offer a prospect can evaluate without confusion.

04 / CONVERSATION

Ask better questions

Ask focused questions, understand the need and recommend a service or package that fits.

05 / OBJECTIONS

Respond without panic

Respond to “it is expensive”, “let me think” and “I will get back to you” without arguing, discounting immediately or disappearing.

06 / CLOSING

Actually ask for the sale

Know how to confirm what the client wants, present the agreed price and invite a clear decision or next step.
